# Patterns

Shared UX rules for every TajERP screen. Follow them so the POS, warehouse,
accounting and CRM apps behave the same way.

## Forms and documents

- **Labels:** one column on phones and two on desktop. Labels go above the
  field (`Label` + control, or `FormItem`). Required fields carry
  `<FormLabel required>`. Never rely on placeholders as labels.
- **Validation:** validate on blur and on submit, not on every keystroke.
  Errors come from `FormMessage`; they are linked to the field through
  `aria-describedby`, and the field gets `aria-invalid`.
- **Field types:**
  - Amounts use `MoneyInput`, quantities `NumberField`, phones `PhoneInput`
    (+992) and dates `DatePicker`. All of them accept "," and "." decimals.
  - Do not use `type="number"`; it rejects the comma on Russian and Tajik
    keyboards.
- **Document lifecycle:** Черновик → Проведён → Отменён. Show the state with a
  `Badge`. Primary actions sit bottom-right ("Провести"), secondary ones as
  `variant="subtle"`. Destructive and irreversible actions ("Отменить чек",
  "Удалить") go through `confirm({ tone: "danger" })`.
- **Long documents:** use a `Stepper` (Документ → Позиции → Проверка) and
  keep tab state with `<Tabs keepMounted>`.

## List → detail

- **Lists:** a `DataTable` with search, sort and pagination. Make rows
  clickable (`onRowClick`) to open the detail. Put bulk actions in the
  selection bar.
- **Opening details:** on desktop, open the detail in a `Sheet` (side="right")
  to keep the list's context. On phones, navigate to a full page. Both use
  `AppShell`.
- **Reports:** use `FilterBar` + `DateRangePicker` presets + `ExportMenu`. Use
  `data-density="compact"` for wide tables, and a totals row (`column.footer`)
  computed over **all filtered rows**.

## Empty, loading and error states

Every list and panel has all three. An empty state says what is missing and
offers the next step:

<div style={{ display: "grid", gap: 16, gridTemplateColumns: "repeat(auto-fit, minmax(260px, 1fr))", margin: "16px 0" }}>
  <EmptyState icon={<PackageOpen />} title="Товаров пока нет" description="Добавьте первый товар или импортируйте каталог из Excel." actionLabel="Добавить товар" onAction={() => {}} />
  <ErrorState title="Не удалось загрузить продажи" description="Сервер не отвечает. Данные на устройстве сохранены." actionLabel="Повторить" onAction={() => {}} />
  <StateView media={<Store />} title="Касса закрыта" description="Откройте смену, чтобы начать продажи." actions={<Button size="sm">Открыть смену</Button>} />
</div>

- **Loading:** use skeletons (`Skeleton`, `DataTable loading`) where the layout
  is known. Use a `LoadingState` spinner only for unknown layouts. Never block
  the POS with a full-screen spinner.
- **Errors:** errors say what happened and what still works, and offer
  "Повторить". Avoid codes and stack traces.

## Offline and sync (POS)

The POS keeps selling without internet. Make the connection state visible but
calm:

<div style={{ display: "flex", flexDirection: "column", gap: 12, margin: "16px 0" }}>
  <Banner tone="warning">Нет соединения — продажи сохраняются на устройстве и отправятся автоматически.</Banner>
  <div style={{ display: "flex", gap: 16, flexWrap: "wrap" }}>
    <SyncStatus status="offline" pending={3} />
    <SyncStatus status="syncing" pending={3} />
    <SyncStatus status="online" lastSyncedAt={Date.now() - 60_000} />
  </div>
</div>

- Use `useOnlineStatus()` + `OfflineIndicator` in the topbar, and a `Banner`
  only while offline.
- Queue work and never drop a sale. Disable only actions that truly need the
  network, such as TijoratAI, SMS and bank QR checks, and say why.
- Self-host fonts (`fonts-local.css`), so a cold start without internet still
  renders correctly.

## TijoratAI: nothing without your "yes"

- Every AI action is a **draft** shown as an `ApprovalCard` with Approve, Edit
  and Reject. The card shows who requested it (AI badge), the amount, the
  affected items and the approval chain.
- Approving returns a Promise. The card shows the loading state, and then a
  toast offers "Отменить" when the action can be undone.
- Every decision lands in the `ActivityTimeline` (audit log).
- Respect roles: hide profit and cost figures from cashiers instead of
  disabling them.

## Touch and density

- On tablets, set `data-density="touch"`: controls are 44px+ high, and table
  rows and calendar days grow too.
- Primary POS actions use `<Button size="xl">`. Quantities use `NumberField`
  steppers or the `Numpad`.
- `BarcodeInput` keeps focus for scanners (`refocus`). Never steal focus while
  the cashier is typing elsewhere.

## Language and money

- Never concatenate strings in components. Use `useMessages` /
  `defineMessages`, and see **Get Started → Localization**.
- Print money through `useFormat().money` (`1 234,50 с`) and right-align
  amounts in tables with `tabular-nums` (`align: "right"` does this).
- Dates are shown as `дд.мм.гггг`; relative time ("5 минут назад") goes in
  feeds, with the exact date in a tooltip or a `<time>` title.
