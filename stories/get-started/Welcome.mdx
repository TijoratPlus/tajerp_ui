**tajerp_ui** is the shared React component library and design-token package
for [Tijorat+](https://www.tijorat.plus) products. That covers the POS (offline
on tablets and phones), stock, money and taxes, customers, staff, branch
dashboards and the TijoratAI assistant.

It ships accessible components (WCAG 2.2 AA), semantic tokens (`--ui-*` CSS
variables) with dark mode and density modes, and component strings in Russian,
Tajik and English. Money is printed in somoni (`1 234,50 с`). Use the
**Theme**, **Language** and **Density** toolbars to preview every story in
each mode.

## What's inside

- **Foundations:** brand greens and warm surfaces, ink scale, status colours,
  chart palette, the `text-ui-*` type scale, density, radii, shadows and
  layering.
- **Primitives:** Button, Input, Select, NumberField, Checkbox, Switch,
  RadioGroup, Toggle, Tabs, Dialog, Sheet, Popover, DropdownMenu, AlertDialog
  with `confirm()`, Tooltip, Toast and more.
- **Data:** DataTable, with sticky header, pinned columns, totals, expandable
  rows, server sort and CSV export, plus Pagination, StatCard, Sparkline,
  DescriptionList and ActivityTimeline.
- **Inputs:** Calendar, DatePicker, DateRangePicker with report presets,
  MultiSelect, Command palette (⌘K), FileUpload, MoneyInput and PhoneInput
  (+992).
- **POS:** ProductTile, CartLine, CartSummary, PaymentSplit (cash, card, Alif
  Pay, Eskhata), Numpad, BarcodeInput and the scanner hook, ReceiptPreview
  (58/80 mm) and SyncStatus/OfflineIndicator.
- **Business and AI:** AppShell, Topbar, BranchSwitcher, LanguageSwitcher,
  FilterBar, ExportMenu, ApprovalCard, ChatBubble and AIAssistantPanel.
- **Entry points:** icons are lucide, from `tajerp_ui/icons`. Charts
  (AxisChart, DonutChart) come from `tajerp_ui/charts`, and react-hook-form
  bindings from `tajerp_ui/form`.
