## Neutrals (ink)

Four text inks. `ink-1` and `ink-2` are for content (7:1+), `ink-3` for meta
text such as timestamps and captions (6:1+ on the background and surfaces in
both themes), `ink-4` for placeholders and disabled text (3:1+). A unit test
(`tests/tokens.test.ts`) recomputes these ratios from `tokens.css`.

## Surfaces and lines

Calm, flat surfaces: the page sits on `--ui-bg` (a near-white wash), cards on
`--ui-surface` with a hairline edge and **no shadow** (`--ui-card-shadow:
none`). Prefer whitespace to separate sections; reach for a line only where
whitespace is not enough. `--ui-hairline-strong` frames inputs and the
composer so they stay visible on the wash.

## Navigation rail

`Sidebar` reads the `--ui-rail-*` tokens. The default dark rail is forest green
with a brand active row. `tone="light"` sets `data-rail="light"`, which maps
the rail to the surface colours with a neutral active row — brand green stays
on primary actions. The light rail follows the theme it is rendered in.

## Semantic runtime tokens

Components read `--ui-*` tokens (see **Get Started → Theming**). They map to the
raw palette above and adapt in dark mode — toggle the Theme toolbar to compare.

## Text-safe colours (contrast)

Every pairing below holds at least **4.5:1** (WCAG AA for normal text) in both
light and dark themes. The base brand green and status hues are for icons,
dots, borders and charts (3:1 is enough there) — **not** for text.

| Use | Utility | Instead of |
|-----|---------|------------|
| Filled surface carrying white text (buttons, active tab/page/segment) | `bg-brand-solid` · hover `bg-brand-solid-hover` | `bg-brand` (3.4:1 under white text) |
| Status text and badges | `text-tj-success-ink` · `text-tj-warning-ink` · `text-tj-error-ink` · `text-tj-info-ink` | `text-tj-success` etc. (1.9–3.1:1) |
| Danger fill under white text | `bg-danger-solid` | `bg-tj-error`, `bg-tj-error-ink` (light coral in dark mode) |
| Captions, meta, table headers | `text-ink-3` (6:1+ on bg/surface) | — |
| Placeholders, disabled text, decorative separators | `text-ink-4` (3:1+) | never for body text |

## Data visualisation

Eight chart hues, led by brand green. They stay distinguishable for the common
colour-vision deficiencies and have their own dark-mode values. AxisChart and
DonutChart use them by default; elsewhere use `bg-chart-N`, `text-chart-N` or
`var(--ui-chart-N)`. Never rely on hue alone: charts also include a legend and
a screen-reader table.
