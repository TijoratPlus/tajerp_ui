## Semantic runtime tokens

Components read `--ui-*` tokens (see **Get Started → Theming**). They map to the
raw palette above and adapt in dark mode — toggle the Theme toolbar to compare.

## Text-safe colours (contrast)

Every pairing below holds at least **4.5:1** (WCAG AA for normal text) in both
light and dark themes. The base brand green and status hues are for icons,
dots, borders and charts (3:1 is enough there) — **not** for text.

| Use | Utility | Instead of |
|-----|---------|------------|
| Filled surface carrying white text (buttons, active tab/page/segment) | `bg-brand-solid` · hover `bg-brand-solid-hover` | `bg-brand` (3.4:1 under white text) |
| Status text and badges | `text-tj-success-ink` · `text-tj-warning-ink` · `text-tj-error-ink` · `text-tj-info-ink` | `text-tj-success` etc. (1.9–3.1:1) |
| Danger fill under white text | `bg-tj-error-ink` | `bg-tj-error` |
| Captions, placeholders, table headers | `text-ink-3` | — (darkened to pass) |
